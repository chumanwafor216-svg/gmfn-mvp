## How to Use This Document

For cooperatives, traders, market associations, seller groups and local enterprise networks.

This is a capability and messaging guide, not the final feature-status authority. Some items are live, some are partial, and some are capability directions that must be checked against the verified self-service guide before public claims or handover.

Core principle: GSN presents organised evidence; the receiving person, organisation or community decides.

Use this copy as an audience-specific conversation pack. It deliberately excludes many modules from the master bank so the receiver sees only what concerns them.

## Capability Modules

## 1. Recognised Community Identity

**Current truth boundary:** Foundational capability; confirm live setup steps in the verified self-service guide before public demonstration.

**The real-life problem:** Many communities live inside WhatsApp names, paper lists and the memory of current leaders. When leadership changes, records and authority become unclear.

**What GSN adds:** GSN gives the community a named domain and governed identity space, separate from ordinary chat noise.

**Why it is important:** Identity is the root of every later action: announcements, meetings, shops, welfare, support and trust evidence.

**Why the reader should care:**

- **For management:** It gives leaders a cleaner process to supervise, delegate, prove and hand over instead of relying only on memory or scattered messages.
- **For members:** It gives ordinary members a clearer way to see, respond, participate, sell, ask, verify or follow up without being trapped in long chat confusion.
- **For the community body:** It turns activity into reusable memory, evidence and opportunity that can survive changing executives, phones and WhatsApp groups.

**The small detail that makes it different:** The quiet difference is continuity: the community is no longer only inside one admin phone.

**What not to overclaim:** GSN presents organised identity evidence; it does not by itself prove legal registration.

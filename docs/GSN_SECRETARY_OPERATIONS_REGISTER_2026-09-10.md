## 8. Evidence Required For Innovation Visa / Innovator Founder Preparation

This register supports evidence gathering only. The actual visa route will need
qualified advice and current official-rule checking.

Evidence GSN must build:

- business plan;
- customer-discovery logs;
- pilot-intent records;
- payment/sponsor willingness evidence;
- market research;
- competitor/differentiation analysis;
- scalable business model;
- founder role and team-capacity plan;
- support-body correspondence;
- product screenshots/demo proof;
- privacy/data-safeguard approach;
- financial forecast and source-of-funds records;
- notes from qualified immigration/university guidance.

Weak evidence to avoid relying on:

- "they liked the idea";
- "they said we should talk again";
- "we sent an email";
- "we attended a session";
- "we know people in the community";
- "we might get grant support";
- "we are building something innovative".

Strong evidence to pursue:

- written pilot intent;
- named organisation problem;
- named budget/sponsor route;
- payment amount or price objection;
- actual usage by admins/members;
- repeat usage after first novelty;
- refusal reasons;
- independent adviser feedback;
- corrected business plan and cash flow;
- support body signposting in writing.

## 9. Secretary Update Protocol

Every time a conversation happens, add:

- date;
- organisation;
- people/roles present;
- channel;
- what they said in their own words;
- problem confirmed;
- current workaround;
- budget/sponsor signal;
- next promised action;
- evidence file or email subject;
- truth boundary.

Never leave a meeting as "good conversation". That phrase is not evidence.

# GSN Community Pilot Participation Agreement - Short Draft

**Date:** [date]

This Pilot Participation Agreement is between:

**GSN / Global Support Network:** [legal name / trading name], represented by Chuma Nwafor.

and

**Community / Organisation:** [community name], represented by [name and role].

Together, they are called **the Parties**.

## 1. Purpose of the pilot

GSN is an early-stage community support and trust platform. The purpose of this pilot is to let the Community use selected GSN tools, give feedback, and help shape how the platform should work for real community needs.

The Parties understand that GSN is still being tested, improved and adjusted. This Agreement is for the pilot only. It is not a promise that every requested feature, integration, API, workflow or custom idea will be completed during the pilot.

## 2. Pilot nature of the service

During the pilot, GSN will provide access to the parts of the platform that are available and suitable for the Community at that time.

Some planned features, APIs, automations, payment tools, verification services or third-party integrations may not yet be available. The Community agrees to exercise reasonable patience while GSN improves the platform and brings more users, communities and partners into the pilot.

GSN will use reasonable care and skill, but the pilot is not a finished enterprise product and may change as learning takes place.

## 3. What is included in the monthly pilot fee

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the monthly pilot fee includes:

- access to the agreed GSN pilot environment;
- use of the GSN features currently activated for the Community;
- ordinary hosting and platform maintenance;
- reasonable technical fault reporting;
- general platform updates made available during the pilot;
- reasonable guidance on how to use available features; and
- pilot feedback and review conversations from time to time.

The monthly fee does not include unlimited personal assistance, full custom build work, guaranteed feature delivery, data entry, travel, third-party charges, paid verification, payment-provider fees, specialist compliance work, or services outside the agreed pilot scope.

## 4. What may be free and what may be paid

GSN may make some features free during the pilot, including ordinary community access, basic community listing or selected basic platform tools.

Other services may be paid or quoted separately, including:

- build-and-handover work;
- detailed setup or configuration beyond ordinary guidance;
- live training or walkthrough sessions;
- premium shop, listing, promotion or marketplace features;
- paid verification or identity checks;
- SMS, email, banking, payment or third-party API charges;
- specialist integrations; and
- work that requires significant extra time from GSN.

GSN will not charge the Community for optional paid services unless the charge has first been explained and approved.

## 5. Pilot fee

The Community will pay a pilot fee of **£25 per month** unless the Parties agree a different amount in writing.

This low pilot fee reflects that GSN is still being developed and that some features and APIs may not yet be fully available.

The fee is payable monthly in advance on or before **[day]** of each month.

If payment is more than 14 days late after written reminder, GSN may pause paid access until the account is brought up to date.

## 6. Extra support and training

If the Community requests live training, walkthroughs or additional hands-on support, the Parties may agree a separate support fee before the work starts.

Suggested pilot support rate:

**£20 per hour, minimum one hour, then £5 per 15 minutes after the first hour.**

The Parties may change this rate in writing if the support needed is larger, more technical or outside normal pilot guidance.

## 7. Changes during the pilot

The Parties expect the pilot to change as learning takes place. GSN may adjust features, layout, workflows, wording, pricing structure or service boundaries during the pilot.

GSN will try to explain material changes clearly and will not knowingly remove core agreed access without reasonable notice, unless needed for security, legal, technical or safeguarding reasons.

Community feedback is welcome, but feedback does not automatically become a required feature or delivery obligation.

## 8. No fixed pilot end date

The pilot does not have a fixed end date at the start because GSN does not yet know how quickly enough communities, users, partners or APIs will be ready.

GSN will give at least **30 days' written notice** before ending the pilot arrangement or moving the Community from pilot pricing to a standard paid plan.

Before the pilot ends or pricing changes, the Parties will discuss available options, which may include:

- continuing on a monthly plan;
- agreeing an annual plan;
- agreeing a longer community plan;
- reducing or increasing the scope; or
- ending the arrangement.

Any post-pilot pricing will be agreed in writing before it applies.

## 9. Possible post-pilot pricing

The Parties understand that GSN may later introduce standard pricing after the pilot.

An initial working expectation may be around **£25 per month per participating community**, but this is not final and may change depending on platform costs, community size, feature use, support level and third-party charges.

If annual or multi-year options are later offered, they will be explained separately. The Community will not be forced into a long-term plan without written agreement.

## 10. Community responsibilities

The Community will:

- appoint an authorised contact or administrator;
- provide accurate information needed for setup;
- decide who may join its community area;
- keep passwords and administrator access secure;
- use GSN lawfully and respectfully;
- avoid uploading unlawful, abusive, discriminatory or unnecessary sensitive material;
- obtain any member permissions it needs; and
- remain responsible for its own leadership, safeguarding, membership and financial decisions.

GSN is a support and coordination platform. It does not replace the Community's own judgement, governance or responsibilities.

## 11. Data and confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other Party's confidential information reasonably secure.

The Community should not upload unnecessary sensitive information, identity documents, children's information, bank details, passwords or private records unless GSN has expressly confirmed that the relevant feature is ready and suitable safeguards have been agreed.

Where personal information is processed, the Parties will act in accordance with applicable UK data protection law. If the pilot begins to involve significant live member data, the Parties may add a simple data-processing schedule before wider rollout.

## 12. Intellectual property

GSN keeps ownership of the GSN platform, software, designs, workflows, Trust Passport, TrustSlip, TrustEvents, methods, documentation, branding and improvements developed during the pilot.

The Community keeps ownership of its own name, logo, documents, member information and original content.

The Community receives a limited right to use the agreed GSN pilot service while this Agreement is active.

## 13. Feedback and pilot evidence

The Community agrees to provide reasonable feedback about what works, what is confusing, and what would make GSN more useful.

GSN may use anonymised or general pilot learning to improve the platform, explain progress to supporters, and support funding or partnership discussions.

GSN will ask for separate permission before publicly naming the Community, publishing a testimonial, or using identifiable member information.

## 14. Service limits

GSN does not provide banking, legal advice, financial advice, regulated identity certification, safeguarding services, debt collection, guaranteed employment, guaranteed funding, or guarantees about any member's conduct.

Any community evidence shown in GSN is intended to support decision-making. It does not replace proper checks, judgement or due diligence.

## 15. Ending or pausing the pilot

Either Party may end this pilot by giving **30 days' written notice**.

Either Party may end or pause access sooner if there is serious misuse, non-payment after reminder, security risk, unlawful use, safeguarding concern, or serious breach that is not corrected after reasonable notice.

When the pilot ends, unpaid approved charges remain payable. The Parties will agree a reasonable process for closing access and returning, exporting or deleting relevant Community information where practical.

## 16. Liability and fairness

Each Party is responsible for losses directly caused by its own serious breach, negligence or unlawful conduct.

Neither Party excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

Because this is a low-cost pilot, GSN is not responsible for indirect losses, loss of profit, loss of opportunity, or losses caused by unavailable third-party services, unfinished APIs, internet outages, user error, or matters outside GSN's reasonable control.

## 17. Agreement changes

Any major change to price, scope, pilot status or responsibilities should be confirmed in writing, including by email or signed note.

Day-to-day platform improvements and normal pilot adjustments do not require a new agreement.

## 18. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the law of Scotland.

The Parties will first try to resolve any disagreement through a good-faith conversation before taking formal action.

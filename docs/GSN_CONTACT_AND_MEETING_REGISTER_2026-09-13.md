This file lists the people, roles, organisations and support routes GSN has

Use it before Business Gateway, RGU, Scottish Enterprise, pilot demos, or any
support conversation where the founder needs to show the effort trail without
exaggerating it.

## Evidence Grade Key

- `Written`: email, document, calendar invite, or attachable written approval exists.
- `Founder-confirmed`: the founder confirms direct conversation/contact happened.
- `Visited`: the founder confirms an in-person visit happened; exact date/place
  should still be recorded if not already in evidence.
- `Pending proof`: contact exists, but a date, role, message, visit detail, or
  reply must still be attached before it becomes strong external evidence.

Founder confirmation is real operating evidence, but it is weaker than an email,
meeting note, signed approval, calendar entry, or third-party reply.

## Devil's Advocate

A long contact list is not traction by itself.

The useful question is not `how many people have we spoken to?` It is:

- who confirmed a painful workflow;
- who has authority;
- who agreed to a next date;
- who may pay or sponsor;
- who corrected the product;
- who gave written permission;
- who can be used safely as evidence.

Until those answers are stronger, the contact list should be presented as
customer-discovery activity, not market validation.

# GSN TrustSlip Evidence and Commercial Working Framework

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** Working synthesis for continued founder brainstorming

## 1. Purpose and authority

This document preserves the principal conclusions, corrections, commercial
hypotheses, dissent, and unresolved questions from the current founder
brainstorming so that the reasoning is not lost or repeatedly restarted.

It does not silently replace the existing mission, Trust Event, TrustSlip,
trusted-commerce, investor, pricing, or forecast protocols. Differences are
recorded so a later approved reconciliation can amend canonical documents
deliberately.

No price, product promise, algorithm, permission, evidence rule, route, schema,
forecast, or corporate strategy becomes final merely because it appears here.

### Truth-status labels

- **Established direction:** consistent with the founder's mission and existing
  protocols.
- **Working definition:** the strongest current explanation, still open to
  precise refinement.
- **Commercial hypothesis:** plausible but requires customer, cost, payment,
  delivery, or renewal evidence.
- **Research question:** cannot be settled by founder preference alone.
- **Constitutional boundary:** a proposed protection that should not be traded
  away merely to obtain revenue.

## 2. Executive synthesis

### 2.1 Original problem

GSN began from a problem observed by the founder over decades: people can build
a good name, relationships, commercial history, contribution, and practical
standing in one place, then lose the visible benefit of that history when they
change location, community, institution, or market.

The founder's experience strongly supports the problem and human mechanism. It
does not, by itself, prove every digital implementation, institutional buyer,
revenue model, or international expansion.

### 2.2 Corrected TrustSlip understanding

**Working definition:** TrustSlip is not GSN's judgement that a person is
trustworthy. It is a purpose-specific presentation of relevant activity
receipts held by GSN. It shows what was recorded, its source, relevant dates,
available confirmation and supporting evidence, and any material corrections
or disputes. The holder reviews the presentation for the intended recipient,
who decides what the evidence supports.

> Use GSN to carry out ordinary activities. GSN keeps structured receipts of
> what happened. When evidence is needed for a new opportunity, GSN organises
> the relevant receipts into a TrustSlip. The recipient examines the evidence
> and makes the decision.

TrustSlip is closer to an organised evidence receipt, case file, or
purpose-built statement than to a score, prediction, character certificate, or
automated decision.

### 2.3 Corrected company understanding

**Working definition:** GSN is an opportunity and activity platform with a
portable evidence ledger. It helps people and organisations express needs,
discover relevant current options, conduct useful commercial, community, and
support activities, preserve structured evidence of what occurred, and prepare
relevant TrustSlips when evidence is needed for another decision.

### 2.4 Three connected engines

1. **Utility engine:** helps people express needs and find relevant, current,
   responsive options.
2. **Evidence engine:** preserves structured receipts and provenance from
   legitimate activity.
3. **Revenue engine:** charges identifiable beneficiaries for defined services
   without selling favourable reputation or human worth.

TrustSlip is the principal portable output of the evidence engine. It is not an
extra activity users must perform merely to look trustworthy.

## 3. Activity-to-opportunity model

> Need -> match -> enquiry -> response -> activity -> outcome -> confirmation
> -> evidence receipt -> relevant TrustSlip -> recipient assessment -> new
> opportunity

Not every journey contains every step. Qualifications, institutional roles,
community responsibilities, contributions, programme completions, or external
records may create evidence through different routes.

The public product model has four layers:

1. activity;
2. structured evidence receipt;
3. purpose-specific TrustSlip presentation; and
4. recipient decision.

The bounded evidence statements described below are an internal projection from
receipts into a presentation. They are not a fifth public product layer.

### Layer 1: activity

Activities may include selling, buying, offering a service, responding to a
request, contributing, holding a responsibility, completing a programme,
fulfilling a commitment, resolving a complaint, providing support, or
presenting an externally issued record.

These activities can happen whether or not TrustSlip exists. TrustSlip does not
cause the past event to become real.

### Layer 2: structured evidence receipt

Where appropriate and permitted, GSN records the activity type, parties, date,
source, context, stated or observed outcome, supporting reference, confirmation
or verification status, dispute or correction status, visibility, and material
limits.

GSN should preserve the minimum evidence necessary for the legitimate purpose.
It should not store every private call, message, or conversation by default.

Automatic capture means that a supported event may generate an accurately
labelled receipt without requiring the user to maintain a separate diary. It
does not mean automatic verification. A submitted request supports only a
request-submitted receipt. A merchant report supports only a
completion-reported-by-merchant receipt. A customer confirmation supports only
a customer-confirmation-received receipt. An introduction, click, or contact
must not silently become a sale, completion, or satisfactory outcome.

Several receipts may describe different stages of one activity. Enquiry,
acceptance, completion report, payment evidence, confirmation, complaint, and
resolution must remain linked to that activity and must not be counted as
several completed transactions.

### Internal evidence statement derived from receipts

An operational record does not automatically become a portable claim. A claim
must say exactly what the evidence supports, such as:

- “The Shop was published on this date.”
- “The merchant replied to these recorded enquiries within this period.”
- “Both parties confirmed that the specified activity occurred.”
- “The institution attested that the person held this defined role.”

The claim must not silently become a judgement of general honesty, competence,
creditworthiness, moral character, or future behaviour.

### Layer 3: TrustSlip presentation

TrustSlip assembles claims relevant to one purpose or recipient. It includes
source, date, context, status, limitations, and an appropriate confirmation
route. It is not the person's entire history.

### Layer 4: recipient decision

The recipient decides authenticity, issuer credibility, relevance, further
checks, process sufficiency, and whether to offer the opportunity. GSN informs
that decision; it does not guarantee acceptance.

## 4. Portable evidence, not portable judgement

“Your good name should travel” remains a valid human expression. The more
precise institutional claim is:

> Relevant evidence of established activity should be easier to preserve,
> present, understand, confirm, update, and reuse when a person enters a new
> relationship or setting.

Five questions must remain separate:

1. **Authenticity:** Is this genuinely the record of the identified source?
2. **Credibility:** Was the source appropriately placed to know or attest to it?
3. **Relevance:** Does the claim help answer the present question?
4. **Acceptance:** Will the recipient use it within the relevant process?
5. **Effect:** Does it reduce repeated work, improve understanding, or help open
   a legitimate opportunity?

Authenticity does not prove credibility. Credibility does not prove relevance.
Relevance does not guarantee acceptance. Acceptance does not prove that
TrustSlip alone caused the final outcome.

## 5. What activities can and cannot establish

| Activity | What it may establish | What it does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|
| Community membership | Recognised relationship or identity continuity | Contribution, honesty, competence, or reliability |
| Defined community role | A scoped responsibility held for a period | Successful performance unless separately evidenced |
| Shop publication | Deliberate commercial presence and published offer | Product quality, sales, or fulfilment |
| Current offer confirmation | Recently stated availability | Future availability or successful delivery |
| DemandBox response | Responsiveness and relevance to a need | Sale, completion, or quality |
| Recorded payment | A payment event was recorded | Satisfactory workmanship or absence of dispute |
| Mutually confirmed completion | Parties agree the activity occurred | Independent corroboration or absence of collusion |
| Complaint resolution | A response and resolution process | That the original complaint was false |
| Wisdom completion | Content or a programme was completed | Broad competence without appropriate assessment |
| Qualification confirmation | The named qualification was checked | Universal suitability or future performance |
| Spotlight purchase | Promotion was purchased | Anything positive about trust or evidence quality |
| Spotlight enquiry | Paid exposure led to a contact | Fulfilment, satisfaction, or sale |

## 6. Evidence provenance and status

Evidence should be described by actual provenance rather than compressed into a
universal scale:

- self-declared;
- directly observed by a specified GSN process;
- confirmed by one counterparty;
- confirmed by the participating parties;
- attested by an identified community or institution;
- supported by an identified independent source;
- verified through a defined authoritative process;
- disputed, corrected, expired, or no longer current.

The issuer's authority is claim-specific. A community may confirm that someone
held a role. It may not be qualified to certify professional competence. GSN
can attest to processes it performed and should preserve external authorship.

The following must remain distinct:

- not supplied;
- not applicable;
- not yet checked;
- insufficient evidence;
- confirmed within a stated scope;
- disputed;
- contradicted by identified evidence;
- expired;
- revoked as a digital credential;
- historically achieved but no longer current.

Missing history must never silently become adverse history.

## 7. Ordinary participation is not automatically a portable dossier

This framework proposes a necessary refinement to broad older phrases such as
“everything becomes evidence.”

GSN may need operational records to provide its services. That does not mean
every click, request, private message, community action, Shop visit, or request
for help should automatically enter a portable reputation history.

Four stages must remain distinct:

1. operational information needed to provide the current service;
2. information used for a specific current task;
3. a deliberately formed evidence claim with provenance and limits;
4. a TrustSlip presentation the holder chooses or is appropriately authorised
   to make for a defined recipient and purpose.

This distinction requires later reconciliation with canonical language that
could be read as converting all participation into TrustSlip evidence.

## 8. Holder agency and evidential integrity

The holder should be able to see claims, understand sources and limits, request
correction, challenge disputed evidence, choose relevant claims, share through
a controlled link or QR, understand recipient access, stop future access where
technically possible, and export appropriate evidence.

Selective presentation must not detach a material limitation from a claim or
represent an expired qualification as current.

A holder may choose relevant receipts, but the packet must say whether it is a
selected collection or a complete recorded history for the stated scope. A
receipt must not be detached from a correction, unresolved dispute, or other
material status necessary to understand it.

Correction is not the same as erasing every unwelcome historical fact. A record
may accurately show an earlier error, its correction, and the current position.

Disabling a future GSN link does not delete a copy already downloaded by a
recipient. Product language must not promise control GSN cannot provide.

## 9. What GSN is and is not deciding

### GSN may

- organise evidence and preserve provenance;
- record process events it actually observes;
- perform clearly defined verification work;
- identify missing, inconsistent, duplicate, stale, or disputed information;
- explain what a claim supports and does not support;
- help recipients locate relevant evidence;
- support proportionate investigation of possible manipulation;
- prepare a purpose-specific TrustSlip.

### AI and imported evidence

AI may retrieve, group, summarise, and explain receipts for a stated purpose.
Every material statement must remain traceable to the receipts behind it, and
unknown facts must remain unknown rather than being inferred into certainty.

External documents may be imported with a clear source label. Holder-supplied
or third-party material must not appear to be an event directly observed or
verified by GSN.

### GSN must not silently

- declare universal trustworthiness or predict whole future behaviour;
- produce a social or moral-worth score;
- treat popularity as reliability or payment as evidence quality;
- treat missing history as negative history;
- make hidden employment, procurement, credit, or support decisions;
- convert private TrustSlip evidence into advertising inventory;
- allow corporations to buy favourable evidence or suppress valid limitations.

> GSN may sell commercial opportunity and evidence-related services. It must
> never sell human worth.

## 11. Matching and recommendation as a commercial capability

Matching belongs at the centre because TrustSlip alone does not make GSN
repeatedly useful.

> Help people resolve expressed needs by finding relevant, available, and
> responsive options while preserving user control and operating within GSN's
> information boundaries and funded capacity.

The engine should separate:

1. **Eligibility:** Is the offer permitted and within scope?
2. **Task relevance:** Does it address the expressed need?
3. **Current constraints:** Are area, timing, status, and availability suitable?
4. **Relevant evidence:** Which bounded facts distinguish suitable options?
5. **Fair opportunity:** Can suitable new entrants be discovered?
6. **Paid allocation:** Is labelled promotion being delivered only among
   independently eligible offers?
7. **Sustainability:** Can GSN provide the service at a supportable cost?

Paid allocation must not silently become organic relevance. Revenue can shape
funded capacity and product investment, but it should not make the highest payer
appear to be the most suitable organic answer.

GSN can understand a current request through categories, synonyms, semantic
retrieval, area, timing, and current availability without permanently
classifying the requester. Current intent can still be sensitive and therefore
requires purpose limits.

### Merchant intelligence

An early assistant may identify unclear descriptions, missing service areas,
stale availability, information buyers repeatedly need, unanswered enquiries,
campaign-offer mismatches, and repeated unmet demand.

It may draft improvements for approval. It must not invent stock,
qualifications, delivery promises, testimonials, prices, or outcomes.

## 12. Information-purpose separation

| Purpose | Appropriate starting use | What it does not authorise |
|---|---|---|
| Resolve a request | Use necessary information within the permitted search scope | Public posting, merchant disclosure, or advertising profiling without authority |
| Select paid promotion | Use the permitted advertising context and eligible public offers | Turning private needs, affiliations, or TrustSlip testimony into ad profiles |
| Report performance | Provide bounded delivery and outcome information | Routine disclosure of identifiable member activity |
| External intelligence | Define a separate buyer, purpose, basis, deliverable, and economics | Treating every GSN record as resale inventory |
| Create TrustSlip | Select relevant legitimate claims for one purpose | Disclosure of the complete person or business history |

When a need is resolved, cancelled, or expired, active recommendations and
notifications should change. Keeping a record for accounting, dispute, fraud,
or legal purposes does not keep the person in an active advertising category.

## 13. Companion authority

1. **Assist privately:** search, explain, compare, draft, or improve an
   unpublished description within the current task.
2. **Act within bounded instruction:** contact specified recipients using an
   approved brief, information set, duration, and spending limit.
3. **Obtain a fresh decision:** before new publication, new recipients, wider
   disclosure, extra spending, or consequential commitments.

The application must enforce those boundaries independently of generated text.
Listings, advertisements, or retrieved pages cannot grant authority to disclose
data, spend credits, or create commitments.

Retries must not create duplicate messages, bookings, publications, or spending.
The user should see what was done and cancel remaining work where possible.

## 14. Commercial definition

GSN does not primarily earn by selling trust or personal data. It may earn when
identifiable beneficiaries purchase:

- Community Domain setup and continuing service;
- sponsored participation, merchant, supplier, or opportunity programmes;
- programme administration and reporting;
- identity, document, qualification, or claim verification work;
- claim issuance, maintenance, correction workflow, expiry, or revocation
  administration;
- merchant and supplier onboarding;
- opportunity distribution and matching workflow;
- clearly labelled Spotlight promotion;
- merchant intelligence and commercial assistance;
- enterprise integrations and APIs where demand exists;
- aggregate purpose-defined reporting and later intelligence products;
- separately priced professional or managed services.

Matching and evidence may improve value, retention, conversion, or efficiency.
That does not automatically create a separate “AI” or “data engine” revenue line.

## 15. Payers and purchase boundaries

Potential payers include merchants, Community Domain owners, corporations,
procurement organisations, employers, councils, associations, development
organisations, foundations, sponsors, and integrating platforms.

These are not one homogeneous corporate market. Each has different budgets,
buying processes, obligations, outcomes, and renewal reasons.

They may buy promotion, programme delivery, workflow, verification,
administration, opportunity distribution, reporting, integration, and
separately governed intelligence.

They must not buy favourable evidence, hidden organic ranking, removal of valid
limitations, predetermined appeals, unrestricted history, private community
surveillance, sensitive targeting, a universal human-worth score, or immunity
from correction and audit.

## 16. Commercial routes under comparison

### Community commerce, Spotlight, and Domain services

**Potential strength:** builds on current activity and direct merchant utility.

**Decisive uncertainty:** activity volume, exact paid benefit, repeat purchase,
delivery cost, and ability to support the wider company.

### Institutional TrustSlip or supplier programmes

**Potential strength:** a defined organisation may pay before GSN has mass
advertising attention and may fund evidence and opportunity testing.

**Decisive uncertainty:** budget owner, purchased benefit, accepted evidence,
bespoke burden, payment timing, renewal, and TrustSlip's added value beyond
training, onboarding, introductions, or document collection.

### Focused combination

A sponsored merchant or supplier programme could fund Shop, matching, and
opportunity activity while testing evidence and reuse. Its risk is bundling so
many services that payment or renewal cannot be attributed.

## 17. Spotlight commercial contract

“Placement” must have an exact meaning. Possible units include a scheduled
booking, entitlement to a rotation for a period, an allocated opportunity, a
recorded render, a measured on-screen placement, or a further interaction.

Attention, enquiry, and sale are separate outcomes. A display does not prove
attention; attention does not prove enquiry; enquiry does not prove sale.

The first experiment should sell the simplest service GSN can explain, deliver,
measure, and reconcile honestly.

Required disciplines:

- define the free baseline before selling “additional” distribution;
- connect bookings to credible capacity;
- state the surface, period, allocation rule, and reporting method;
- distinguish low audience activity from GSN underdelivery;
- keep paid and organic exposure distinguishable;
- reconcile delivery from GSN records;
- provide a remedy useful to the buyer;
- record restored credits as continuing obligations;
- avoid treating a different payer as a different underlying product unless the
  purchased service actually differs.

## 18. Community Domain packaging

Community Domain can be modelled through explicit alternatives:

- setup fee;
- monthly service;
- annual service;
- setup plus recurring service;
- sponsored or institution-funded service.

Each alternative must identify initial work, continuing obligations, support,
infrastructure, payment timing, cancellation, renewal, inclusions, beneficiary,
and payer. Alternatives must not be blended and treated as one proven offer.

## 19. TrustSlip pilot design

A recipient-led pilot should identify four roles:

| Role | Required question |
|---|---|
| Holder | Whose evidence is represented and what opportunity is sought? |
| Payer | Who buys which service, from which budget, and for what benefit? |
| Issuer | Who is accountable for each claim and supporting evidence? |
| Opportunity owner | Who can consider the evidence in a real decision? |

One organisation may occupy several roles, but the roles should not be confused.

Before the pilot, the opportunity owner should identify the requirement, claim
it will examine, credible issuer or checking process, possible effect on the
process, other required evidence, and what the current process accepts.

The key comparison is:

> With equivalent underlying evidence and support, does TrustSlip make the
> information easier to understand, authenticate, update, correct, reuse, or
> assess than the recipient's current method?

Acceptance inside one programme demonstrates workflow value. Reuse by another
recipient tests portability beyond the originating relationship.

## 20. AI boundaries

AI may assist with language understanding, matching, evidence organisation,
missing information, duplicates, inconsistencies, stale records, claim
explanations, merchant improvements, and proportionate investigation.

AI must not silently infer universal character; fabricate evidence, stock,
qualifications, testimonials, or outcomes; convert suspicion into a fraud
finding; make final high-consequence decisions in an early pilot; hide
sponsorship; act outside bounded authority; repeatedly interrupt after task
resolution; or build opaque permanent profiles merely because data exists.

A flag is not a finding. A recommendation is not a guarantee. A generated
explanation is not evidence unless grounded in identified records.

## 21. Revenue development is conditional

### Early possibilities

- paid sponsored programmes;
- Community Domain setup or continuing service;
- managed onboarding and verification;
- merchant activation and promotion;
- implementation and professional services;
- bounded Spotlight experiments.

### After repeatable demand

- recurring enterprise or Domain licences;
- standard programme packages;
- claim lifecycle administration;
- verification usage;
- supplier or workforce onboarding;
- paid opportunity distribution;
- aggregate reporting.

### After integration demand and maturity

- verification APIs;
- embedded TrustSlip integrations;
- enterprise workflow subscriptions;
- partner-operated programmes;
- aggregate benchmarks;
- contextual corporate promotion;
- white-label or international licensing.

Calendar years do not create these products. Each transition requires a buyer,
repeatable workflow, acceptable cost, continuing use, and willingness to pay.
One contract must not be counted several times because it contains onboarding,
verification, reporting, promotion, and programme management.

## 22. Free core and paid work

The mission supports accessible ordinary participation, basic evidence access,
source and status information, correction and dispute routes, basic
purpose-specific sharing, privacy controls, and appropriate access information.

Possible paid work includes a new verification, new defined attestation,
continuing current-status checking, programme administration, enterprise
workflow, integration, enhanced promotion, and specialised support.

GSN should charge for defined work and continuing service value. Evidence
integrity, appropriate correction routes, and applicable holder rights must not
depend on payment.

True historical achievements should not be given artificial expiry merely to
manufacture recurring fees. Current status may legitimately require renewal.

## 23. Forecast and break-even discipline

A conditional five-year forecast can be built before every assumption is
proven, but estimates must remain distinguishable from evidence.

The monthly model should show contract value, bookings, invoices, receivables,
cash collected, deferred obligations, recognised revenue, direct delivery cost,
contribution, shared operating cost, operating result, cash movement, and
continuing credit or refund obligations.

Founder labour requires two distinct views where relevant:

- actual founder cash remuneration;
- replacement-cost or economic assessment.

The same work must not be counted twice.

Scenario inputs include sales cycle, contract value, payment timing, participant
volume, delivery hours, evidence-item cost, verification, correction, dispute,
fraud, security, support, Spotlight capacity, matching and AI cost, conversion,
renewal, founder-dependence reduction, acquisition cost, and pre-receipt funding.

TrustSlip cost drivers should separately include supported activities generating
receipts, fields and attachments retained per receipt, activity and participant
linking, correction and dispute workload, packet preparation and sharing
frequency, assembly processing, and retention periods. These can be estimated
conditionally, but a precise cost per receipt must not be invented before
observed workflow and usage exist.

Break-even must be named precisely: transaction contribution, customer
contribution, product line, operating, cash, economic including founder
replacement, or cumulative capital recovery.

Product profitability does not establish company solvency. All propositions
share one cash position, staff capacity, reputation, and risk structure.

## 24. Metrics that matter

### Utility and matching

- proportion of genuine requests receiving suitable options;
- irrelevant, unavailable, or out-of-area result rate;
- unresolved requests with no suitable result;
- useful response and requester-reported resolution;
- repeat use for another genuine need;
- cost per useful connection.

### Merchant and promotion

- current and complete offer rate;
- relevant enquiries and properly contextualised response time;
- understood paid-service purchase;
- delivery against the purchased promise;
- underdelivery and remedy rate;
- repeat Spotlight purchase;
- merchant support cost;
- paid versus organic exposure.

### TrustSlip and evidence

- claims with complete provenance and limits;
- correction, dispute, appeal, and confirmed manipulation rates;
- time to correction;
- recipient use in a real process;
- reduction in repeated document requests or assessment time;
- presentation to a second appropriate recipient;
- legitimate opportunity influenced by the evidence;
- holder understanding of what TrustSlip does and does not establish.

### Institutional commercial evidence

- named budget owner;
- paid contract rather than only interest;
- cash collection and actual delivery cost;
- participant benefit and recipient acceptance;
- renewal, expansion, or follow-on purchase;
- declining founder intervention;
- standardisation and margin development.

Clicks, views, records created, QR codes shared, and TrustSlips issued are not
sufficient outcome measures by themselves.

## 25. Investor explanation

The system is complex to build but should not be complex to explain or use.

> GSN is an opportunity platform with an automatic evidence ledger. People and
> small businesses carry out ordinary commercial and community activities. GSN
> preserves structured receipts they can later organise into a TrustSlip when
> they need to demonstrate relevant experience or history to another party.

For an enterprise audience:

> GSN helps institutions discover, onboard, and assess people or small suppliers
> using purpose-specific evidence generated or preserved through real activity,
> while the holder retains appropriate control and the institution makes the
> final decision.

The company must not be pitched as many simultaneous businesses. The first
investment proposition needs one payer, one product, one evidence purpose, one
recipient workflow, one principal milestone, and one review date.

The strongest proof is not issuing TrustSlips. It is a relevant recipient using
a specific claim in a real process, the holder receiving legitimate benefit,
the payer receiving defined value, and the service being deliverable and
renewable at supportable economics.

## 26. Defensible advantage

The moat should not be user captivity or evidence that cannot leave GSN.

Potential advantages include:

- a useful and accepted evidence vocabulary;
- issuer and recipient relationships;
- strong provenance and claim boundaries;
- correction, dispute, expiry, and revocation workflows;
- fraud and collusion controls;
- embedded enterprise processes;
- superior matching and opportunity workflows;
- permissioned evidence continuity;
- accumulated outcome knowledge;
- institutional confidence in GSN governance;
- continuing utility from Shops, DemandBox, Domains, and programmes.

Portability may reduce lock-in. GSN should earn through continuing workflow
value, verification, coordination, opportunity, service, and promotion.

## 27. Constitutional boundaries proposed for ratification

1. No universal trust or human-worth score.
2. No payment for favourable evidence.
3. No hidden conversion of paid placement into organic suitability.
4. No use of private TrustSlip evidence as advertising inventory.
5. No treatment of missing history as adverse history.
6. No automatic conversion of every interaction into a portable employability
   or procurement dossier.
7. No material claim without provenance, scope, date, status, and limits.
8. No correction, dispute, or appeal outcome determined by payment.
9. No corporate access to unrestricted personal history.
10. No hidden AI judgement of universal trustworthiness.
11. No claim that an activity receipt proves more than it supports.
12. No promise that portable evidence guarantees acceptance.
13. No suppression of material limitations in selective sharing.
14. No revenue model dependent on surveillance, sensitive targeting, or private
    community exploitation.

## 28. Principal risks and devil's-advocate findings

### Problem truth is not business-model proof

Lived experience strongly supports the problem. It does not prove institutional
payment, recipient acceptance, cross-border transfer, verification economics,
or renewal.

### TrustSlip may not be the first service purchased

The first paid product may be programme administration, supplier onboarding,
Community Domain service, promotion, or verification. TrustSlip may be the
evidence output within that product.

### Supporting services can conceal TrustSlip's contribution

Training, document collection, improved descriptions, introductions, and staff
assistance may create the opportunity even if TrustSlip adds little.

### A receipt can still be misinterpreted

Recipients may treat a large portfolio as a de facto score or demand complete
disclosure. Purpose limits and selective, integrity-preserving presentation
remain necessary.

### Evidence can be manufactured

Valuable evidence creates incentives for fake enquiries, staged transactions,
reciprocal confirmations, paid attestations, suppressed complaints, and
collusion.

### Corporate funding can distort the mission

Corporations may value compliance, exclusion, or surveillance more than holder
opportunity. A large contract is not product-market fit if it requires mission
corruption.

### Product breadth can prevent proof

GSN can be coherent while validating too many products at once. Each funded
experiment needs one principal commercial milestone.

### Technology sophistication is not commercial value

AI, semantic search, credential formats, APIs, analytics, and portability are
capabilities. Their value must appear through outcomes, reduced cost, payment,
renewal, or defensibility.

## 29. Kill, pause, and redesign criteria

A claim, programme, or commercial corridor should be paused or redesigned if:

- relevant recipients do not use the claim;
- it does not improve understanding, reuse, assessment effort, or opportunity;
- activity-derived evidence is too easy to manufacture;
- holders do not understand its limits;
- meaningful demand requires prohibited surveillance or scoring;
- correction, dispute, fraud, or verification costs remain uneconomic;
- issuers will not accept responsibility for claims;
- programmes remain entirely founder-dependent;
- customers fund pilots but do not renew or expand;
- TrustSlip cannot be separated from paid promotion;
- lack of GSN history becomes an unfair barrier;
- the company must become a credit or social-scoring system to survive.

Failure of one corridor does not automatically disprove the original TrustSlip
mission. It may disprove that claim family, buyer, workflow, or delivery model.

## 30. Current truth map

### Established direction

- GSN exists to preserve and mobilise value people and communities create.
- Evidence comes from activity; TrustSlip communicates relevant evidence.
- TrustSlip must not become a universal character or trust score.
- Community, commerce, requests, contribution, and opportunity are connected.
- TrustSlip should show evidence limits, not only positive claims.
- Ordinary participation should remain affordable.
- Paid promotion must remain distinct from evidence quality.

### Strong working definitions

- TrustSlip is a purpose-specific presentation of relevant activity receipts.
- GSN preserves structured receipts; recipients decide their meaning.
- GSN has utility, evidence, and revenue engines.
- Matching and recommendation are core to recurring usefulness.
- The company should earn through defined services, not human worth.

### Commercial hypotheses

- corporations or institutions can become principal payers;
- sponsored merchant or supplier programmes may be an effective early corridor;
- Spotlight can become repeatable merchant revenue;
- Community Domain can become recurring organisational service;
- matching and merchant intelligence can improve retention and conversion;
- enterprise verification, workflow, APIs, and integrations can scale later.

### Current research questions

## 31. Immediate learning agenda

### Test A: one recurring need and matching process

Choose one real need, identify offers, compare current search with transparent
or assisted matching, and measure suitability, response, resolution, cost, and
fairness.

### Test B: one defined Spotlight promise

Choose one surface, define the free baseline and paid benefit, choose rotation
entitlement or counted delivery, and measure capacity, delivery, reporting,
remedies, usefulness, and repeat purchase.

### Test C: one recipient-led evidence claim

Identify holder, payer, issuer, and opportunity owner; define one claim; compare
TrustSlip with the current process; and measure comprehension, repeated-work
reduction, correction, acceptance, opportunity effect, cost, and payment.

### Test D: one Community Domain package

Define setup and continuing service separately, record workload, and test buyer
value, payment timing, support, renewal, and contribution.

Tests may be combined only where purposes remain clear and each component's
contribution can still be interpreted.

## 32. Final working statement

GSN should not be defined as a platform that decides whom society should trust.

It should help people carry out useful activities, keep structured and governed
receipts of what happened, connect present needs to suitable opportunities, and
organise relevant evidence into a TrustSlip when a recipient needs to understand
the holder's experience or history. The recipient remains responsible for the
decision.

The commercial ambition is to maximise defensible revenue from parties who
benefit from promotion, workflow, verification, programme administration,
opportunity distribution, integration, and intelligence, so that ordinary
participation and basic evidence rights remain free or inexpensive.

> GSN keeps and organises the receipts of legitimate activity. It may help
> those receipts open opportunity. It must not sell a judgement of human worth.

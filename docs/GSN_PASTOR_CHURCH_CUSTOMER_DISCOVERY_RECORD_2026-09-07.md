## 2. Current Workflow

The church uses multiple WhatsApp groups across different church units.

Confirmed workflow:

- official messages are sent by the church admin or main church phone;
- event notices and registration links are posted across WhatsApp groups;
- leadership depends on responses, registrations, or engagement to know whether
  members acted;
- there is no clean way to know who read, clicked, responded, or still needs
  follow-up.

## 3. Pain Points Named

The first challenge is coordination and engagement visibility across WhatsApp.

Specific pain:

- important notices and event links may be posted but engagement can remain low;
- leaders cannot tell whether people missed the message, saw it and did not
  act, or had difficulty using the link;
- weekly attendance and member follow-up are important but not systematically
  captured;
- regular and new member follow-up can require guesswork.

## 4. Preferred First Pilot Workflow

The strongest first pilot discussed was attendance memory plus timely care
follow-up.

Possible pilot:

- mixed group of about 50 adults;
- Sunday QR attendance check-in;
- QR codes placed around the church, for example seats, rows, entrances, or
  other easy locations;
- members scan to mark attendance without creating entrance traffic;
- authorised church leaders see a private snapshot after service;
- regular members who miss service can be privately flagged for care follow-up.

## 5. Authority And Follow-Up

The first follow-up should sit with the Welcome Team or Follow-up Team, not
necessarily the pastor directly.

Possible follow-up route:

- phone call;
- text message;
- WhatsApp message;
- home visit if concern continues;
- pastoral escalation if needed;
- welfare/care escalation if needed.

## 6. Privacy Boundary

Personal and sensitive information must remain private and visible only to
authorised church people.

Sensitive information mentioned:

- date of birth;
- bank information;
- home address;
- email address;
- phone number where consent has not been given;
- other personal details.

GSN must not present this workflow as pastoral counselling, safeguarding,
spiritual judgement, payment reconciliation, or public exposure of member
attendance.

## 7. Success Measure

After two weeks, the pilot would be useful if the church can clearly know:

- the number of regular members who missed service;
- which regular members missed service;
- whether the Welcome Team or Follow-up Team received prompts at the right time;
- whether necessary follow-up could begin without guessing.

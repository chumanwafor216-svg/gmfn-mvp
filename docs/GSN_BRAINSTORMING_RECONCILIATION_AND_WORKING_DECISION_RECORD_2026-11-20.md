Date: 20 November 2026
Status: reconciled management record; not an implementation, payment, forecast, or deployment authorization

## 1. Reconciliation verdict

The supplied `GSN Brainstorming Summary and Working Decision Record` is substantially aligned with the existing GSN commercial, Spotlight, financial-engine, and TrustSlip records.

It does not require a strategic reversal. Its strongest contribution is to combine the existing conclusions into one connected system and to add clearer pilot, payer, forecasting, and business-plan boundaries.

The combined position is:

> GSN is a community commerce and opportunity network whose scalable economic direction is promotion-led mixed income. Shops, public discovery, Demand Box, matching, Spotlight, Community Domain, and TrustSlip form one connected system. Spotlight is the leading paid experiment. TrustSlip is the purpose-specific presentation of relevant activity evidence, not a purchased trust score, endorsement, or guarantee.

That statement is a working strategic definition. It does not prove demand, repeat purchase, contribution margin, institutional acceptance, safe scale, or break-even.

## 2. Truth-status hierarchy

When the source records use different language, apply these statuses:

1. **Settled working decision** — use for product planning and conditional modelling unless the founder deliberately changes it.
2. **Implementation pending** — the intended behaviour is sufficiently defined, but code, operations, migration, or controls are incomplete.
3. **Recommendation awaiting adoption** — a reasoned proposal, not yet a founder-approved commercial rule.
4. **Evidence question** — only observed customer, delivery, cost, or outcome data can answer it.
5. **Founder ambition or reported relationship** — preserve it, but do not present it externally as validated market evidence, endorsement, contract, or revenue.

The latest explicit founder correction takes precedence over older summaries. A new planning choice must not silently rewrite historical balances, funded obligations, privacy expectations, or published records.

## 4. Genuine additions from the supplied record

The following should be carried forward because they sharpen the existing summary:

### 4.1 Separate access, participation, and payer

The organisation that introduces or hosts participants is not automatically the payer. An individual merchant may buy promotion even when the merchant's organisation has not bought Community Domain. Sponsored participation and merchant-funded promotion test different commercial propositions and must be measured separately.

### 4.2 Initial merchant-pilot recommendation

The recommended first promotion cohort is responsive merchants and service providers with current offers in a concentrated Scottish community market, beginning with relationships the founder can realistically activate. This is a pilot recommendation, not proof that Scotland is the final or largest market.

The wider immigrant and diaspora ambition remains intact. Different regions must not be assumed to have interchangeable acquisition cost, willingness to pay, support burden, or retention.

### 4.3 Demand Box charging remains unresolved

Low-friction need creation and relevant response remain the direction. Exact free quotas, response limits, and any post-pilot charges were not consistently settled. Older one-reply or two-reply rules must not be silently promoted into the final package.

### 4.4 Free participation needs a cost owner

Accessible identity, profile maintenance, browsing, basic Shop participation, ordinary evidence capture, and useful information can support adoption. Their usefulness does not make their delivery cost disappear. The financial model must identify whether promotion margin, Community Domain, institutional programmes, or another payer funds the free layer.

### 4.5 Forecast accounting boundaries

The model must distinguish cash received, earned revenue, unused service obligations, refunds, restored credits, community allocations, company costs, and merchant turnover. Credit purchase and credit consumption are not two sales. Sponsor funding and the resulting merchant entitlement are not two revenues.

### 4.6 Founder effort must be visible

Actual founder cash pay and the replacement-cost value of unpaid founder work should be shown separately. Manual pilot support is legitimate, but it must be measured so that exceptional founder effort is not mistaken for a scalable operating model.

### 4.7 Business-plan evidence discipline

Reported RGU, One Tech Hub, Converge, or other support relationships must be verified before external use. Advice, training, research interest, a memorandum, endorsement, contract, and paid customer are different facts.

## 5. Status corrections required

The supplied record is strong, but the following language must not be read more broadly than the evidence supports.

### 5.1 Promotion-led does not mean promotion-proven

Calling promotion the leading commercial experiment or primary scalable direction is justified as a management choice. Calling GSN an established advertising business, a superior advertising machine, or a dependable promotion-revenue engine is not yet justified.

### 5.2 TrustSlip remains central without becoming a separate compulsory fee

TrustSlip is central to GSN's social and institutional purpose because it preserves and presents relevant evidence. That does not establish a standalone TrustSlip market. Embedded-first treatment remains a recommendation; substantial additional verification or preparation may later be sold when a payer, benefit, cost, and obligation are clear.

### 5.3 Five-view intent and enforcement must remain distinguishable

The interpretation that five separate occasions equal five impressions is settled. Suppressing a sixth automatic display is the current protocol target, but identity resolution, authoritative day, community scope, deliberate Shop browsing, and enforcement behaviour still require verified implementation and policy boundaries.

### 5.4 Daypart allocation is settled; timezone administration is not

The 10/10/10 allocation and proposed 05:00-12:00, 12:00-18:00, and 18:00-00:00 windows are the working design. The authoritative service timezone, daylight-saving handling, late booking, and partial-day treatment remain open operational rules.

### 5.5 Overnight promotion is not part of the paid promise

The proposed 00:00-05:00 free-promotion allocation remains a proposal. It must not consume or be used to make good the paid daypart entitlement unless a later explicit policy says so.

### 5.6 Public discovery is not forced exposure

Every deliberately published active public offer should be eligible for discovery by default. This does not mean every Shop record, private community record, contact detail, TrustSlip, or historical record is forcibly public. Owner controls and legacy-record reconciliation remain essential.

### 5.7 Corporate subsidy is a strategic aim, not a guaranteed funding fact

The aim that corporate and institutional income should keep ordinary participation inexpensive or free is coherent. It remains conditional on real buyers, viable delivery cost, renewal, and sufficient margin.

### 5.8 Legal and corporate labels require verification

The attachment labels the company `Global Support Network Ltd`. That wording should be checked against current corporate records before external publication. This reconciliation does not certify legal name, legal status, tax treatment, or regulatory position.

## 6. Reconciled commercial architecture

### Layer A — accessible participation

Identity, community participation, eligible public browsing, one useful Shop direction, direct sharing, permitted Demand Box activity, and ordinary evidence capture. Exact limits and operating cost remain to be defined.

### Layer B — merchant promotion

Pipeline Credits fund scheduled Spotlight opportunities under one booking, capacity, fulfilment, adjustment, and reporting contract.

### Layer C — sponsored opportunity distribution

An organisation may fund a bounded merchant cohort or campaign. Sponsor funding must use the same authoritative entitlement and delivery system without creating duplicate revenue or duplicate credits.

### Layer D — Community Domain and institutional service

Named organisational space, specified administration and governance, records, programme workflow, reporting, support, verification, and integration where a defined buyer values them. Package and price remain recommendations until obligations and costs are complete.

### Layer E — later intelligence

Aggregate, permissioned, privacy-safe reporting or optimisation may become a service only after buyer, lawful purpose, deliverable, cost, and governance are established. Private TrustSlip evidence, sensitive needs, affiliations, or private community activity must not become advertising inventory.

## 7. Final decision register

### Settled working decisions

- GSN's strategic economic direction is promotion-led mixed income, while its social and institutional foundation remains evidence continuity and TrustSlip.
- Deliberately published active public offers are discoverable by default with owner controls.
- Pipeline Credits are the intended ordinary merchant Spotlight rail.
- GBP 0.50 buys one credit and the working 30-opportunity package.
- The package uses a 10/10/10 daypart allocation.
- Scheduled opportunity is the purchased unit; impressions and outcomes are measured results.
- There is no five-person audience target or ceiling.
- Additional purchases do not reset the same viewer's frequency count.
- Ten seconds is a scheduling baseline, not a separate tariff.
- Conditional five-year promotion scenarios are permitted when assumptions are explicit.
- Payment never buys trust, endorsement, stronger evidence, or a favourable TrustSlip conclusion.

## 9. Final devil's-advocate conclusion

GSN now has a coherent strategic package and a defined initial promotion unit. It does not yet have a proven scalable business model.

The strongest current claim is not that GSN will outperform Facebook, Amazon, WhatsApp, or established advertising networks. The defensible claim is narrower: GSN combines community entry, public commerce discovery, paid promotion, demand expression, measurable activity, and portable purpose-specific evidence in a distinctive way that is ready for bounded testing.

The next source of truth must be customer and operating evidence: useful discovery, real payment, fulfilment, repeat purchase, retention, delivery cost, and recipient use of evidence. More conceptual breadth cannot substitute for those results.

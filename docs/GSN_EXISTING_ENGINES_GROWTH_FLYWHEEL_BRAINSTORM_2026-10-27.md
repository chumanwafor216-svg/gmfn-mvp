# GSN Existing-Engines Growth Flywheel Brainstorm - 2026-10-27

Status: owner brainstorm captured for later strategy work; no runtime
implementation, new engine, reward, advertising entitlement or payment change
is authorised by this document.

## Owner's central direction

GSN does not need to respond to every growth idea by inventing a new engine.
Most of the engines and buttons already exist. The work is to study proven
models elsewhere, identify what fits GSN, and compose existing capabilities
into enough real-life reasons for people to:

1. come to GSN;
2. receive or pursue something useful;
3. buy, sell, belong, organise, achieve, invite or be recognised;
4. return because the useful activity continues;
5. bring other people who share the activity;
6. create a concentrated, measurable and appropriately governed audience that
   advertisers may eventually pay to reach.

The intended principle is:

```text
REUSE -> COMPOSE -> ATTRACT -> ACTIVATE -> RETAIN -> SHARE -> MEASURE -> MONETISE
```

Do not substitute:

```text
INVENT MORE FEATURES -> ASSUME PEOPLE WILL COME -> ASSUME ADVERTISERS WILL PAY
```

## The four first-door expectations

A person arriving at GSN should be able to recognise at least one honest reason
for coming:

| Expectation | Existing GSN capability families to inspect and compose |
|---|---|
| Buy or find something useful | Marketplace, Shop/Public Shop, DemandBox, Spotlight, Protected Trade and trust evidence |
| Sell, promote or earn through genuine trade | Shop Control, Shop Assets, Spotlight, media preparation, Shop Analytics, Merchant Verification and TrustSlip |
| Belong, invite friends or organise a real community | entry/invite/QR flows, Community Home, Community Domain, membership, notices, confirmations and governance |
| Achieve, prove or unlock an opportunity | TrustPassport, TrustSlip, Merchant Verification, contribution/behaviour evidence, referrals, introductions and opportunity guidance |

These are entrance motives, not guaranteed outcomes. GSN must not imply that
joining guarantees money, buyers, work, approval, trust or opportunity.

## Acquisition, activation and retention are different jobs

### Acquisition: why come?

- a specific item, service, request or opportunity;
- a trusted community invitation;
- a place to organise an existing association;
- a way to make a shop or capability visible;
- a portable TrustSlip or merchant-verification need;
- a direct referral from a known person;
- a useful creative/promotion tool that turns ordinary phone media into a clear
  business presentation.

### Activation: what useful thing happens first?

- join or create the intended community;
- publish one real shop item;
- find or respond to one relevant request;
- create one clear Spotlight presentation;
- verify or request one bounded trust record;
- invite one real participant;
- complete one task that produces visible value.

A registration without one of these outcomes is not meaningful activation.

### Retention: why return?

- new requests, responses, opportunities or community decisions;
- Shop and Spotlight activity that can be understood honestly;
- Market Wisdom and owner guidance pointing to the next useful step;
- notifications and reminders tied to unfinished real work;
- updated trust/merchant evidence;
- repeat customers, repeat community activity or repeat operational value;
- saved time in organising the same group or business activity.

Visual polish may help adoption, but it cannot replace repeat utility.

## Phone-media-to-promotion direction

The owner does not want GSN to depend on employing content creators to visit
every shop. A seller should be able to use an ordinary phone to capture a
photo or video and let GSN help turn it into a clearer business presentation.

Future investigation should first inventory and compose the existing:

- photo/frame and image-preparation controls;
- video preparation and sound/editing controls;
- Shop Assets;
- Spotlight media and message fields;
- campaign renderer and GSN-HOUSE presentation machinery;
- public shop identity, contact action and branded presentation surfaces.

Candidate outcome:

```text
Phone photo/video
  -> simple GSN-assisted crop/clean-up/template
  -> shop name + short offer + contact/action
  -> preview
  -> owner approval
  -> Shop and/or Spotlight publication
```

This should remain a small-business promotion assistant, not an attempt to
become a full professional studio or a new general-purpose video platform.
Do not build a larger media engine until the inventory proves the existing
tools cannot produce the approved outcome.

## Growth Partner and sharing loop

The existing network-growth attribution spine can support later direct
introduction evidence. The future model may connect:

```text
Useful GSN outcome
  -> member/owner shares or invites
  -> participant enters through an attributable source
  -> participant confirms the relationship
  -> participant reaches a real activation milestone
  -> GSN measures retention and qualifying paid value
  -> only then can a governed reward be considered
```

This does not authorise a commission or reward. It keeps the distinction between
attribution, causation, activation and earned revenue.

## Advertiser value comes last, not first

Advertisers do not automatically arrive because many accounts exist. A
credible advertising proposition needs:

- a concentrated audience with a known permitted context;
- evidence that people return for a real activity;
- a clear placement/product and truthful qualification rule;
- brand safety and community governance;
- consent and privacy boundaries;
- measured delivery/attention rather than issued-campaign counts;
- an honest action or conversion path;
- transparent pricing, billing, refunds and reporting;
- no cross-community private-data leakage.

GSN already has Spotlight, GSN-HOUSE, campaign/presentation and attention
foundations to inspect. The later job is to package and price the appropriate
existing capability, not assume that every member surface should become an ad
surface.

## Proposed flywheel to test

```text
Real need or gain
  -> Buy / Sell / Belong / Organise / Achieve
  -> First useful action
  -> Repeat value and evidence
  -> Share / Invite / Refer
  -> More relevant participants
  -> More useful supply, demand and community activity
  -> Measurable permitted audience
  -> Paid promotion or other module revenue
  -> Reinvestment in utility, trust and growth
```

Every arrow is a hypothesis until GSN measures it. A large top-of-funnel with
weak activation or retention is not a healthy flywheel.

## Unabated-truth boundaries

- People do not stay merely because GSN has many buttons.
- Better-looking photos or videos do not themselves create buyer demand.
- Reactions and views are not customers or revenue.
- Community membership is not automatically commercial intent.
- TrustSlip or Merchant Verification is not a guarantee of a sale, job, loan or
  external acceptance.
- Advertisers will require evidence, targeting boundaries and credible
  reporting; audience size alone is weak evidence.
- Rewards introduced before durable revenue can drain cash and attract fraud.
- Copying another platform's visible feature without its distribution,
  economics, governance and user habit can reproduce cost without reproducing
  growth.
- The safest fast-growth route is to make one existing GSN outcome genuinely
  useful, repeatable, shareable and measurable before widening the loop.

## Facilitator Collection Rule

Do not treat this sheet as adoption evidence by itself.

It is useful only if it reveals:

- one real ICA decision;
- whether the strongest pain is administrative, verification, economic, or community memory;
- where the evidence lives today;
- who should confirm it;
- what must stay private;
- what would make ICA refuse;
- whether GSN sounds useful, unnecessary, risky, or unclear after the explanation;
- which one workflow they would actually test first;
- which attractive ideas must be treated honestly as future capability.

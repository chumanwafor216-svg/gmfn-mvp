## Purpose

Prepare for a focused product discovery workshop with Pastor Mrs. The aim is to
understand the real church/community workflow, test which GSN products fit, and
identify whether a narrow pilot could be useful.

This is discovery, not a sales close. Do not claim adoption, church approval,
board approval, payment, endorsement or permission to use private member data
unless Pastor Mrs gives that clearly and it is recorded in writing.

## Best Fit Products To Explore

Keep the discussion narrow. The strongest likely products are:

- Community Bulletin for official church notices and acknowledgement;
- QR attendance or simple attendance memory for Sunday/service follow-up;
- Focus Commitments for volunteers, event support, promises and deadlines;
- Demand Box for care/support requests that need private follow-up;
- Meeting Pack for committee, welcome-team or follow-up-team continuity;
- Member ID / verification only if membership/currentness is a real problem;
- Pilot Participation Agreement only if she is ready to discuss a structured pilot.

## Suggested 35-Minute Flow

1. Open with the reason for the workshop: understand the current workflow before
   proposing a pilot.
2. Ask what currently happens when official notices, event links, attendance or
   member follow-up are managed through WhatsApp or paper.
3. Ask where the pain is strongest: missed notices, low responses, attendance
   uncertainty, newcomer follow-up, volunteer promises, welfare/care follow-up,
   records, or leadership continuity.
4. Show only the matching GSN surface or simple example. Do not give a full app
   tour.
5. Ask what would make a two-week or 8-12 week pilot useful enough to continue.
6. If there is clear fit, ask who has authority to approve a pilot, payment, or
   sponsor-backed support.
7. End by agreeing one clear next action and whether a written summary should be
   sent for correction.

## Questions To Ask

- What church or community workflow wastes the most time today?
- Who currently sends official notices and who follows up non-response?
- How do you know who saw a notice, registered, attended, or needs care?
- What information must remain private and who is allowed to see it?
- Which team would operate this in practice: pastor, secretary, welcome team,
  follow-up team, welfare team, youth team, or committee?
- What would count as success after two weeks?
- If the pilot worked, who would decide whether the church pays, a sponsor pays,
  or the pilot stops?

## Devil's Advocate

A warm workshop is not validation by itself. The useful evidence is whether she
names a repeated workflow pain, confirms who owns the workflow, identifies what
must stay private, and gives a specific next action or pilot authority route.

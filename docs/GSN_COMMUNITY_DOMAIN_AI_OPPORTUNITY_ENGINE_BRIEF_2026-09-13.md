# GSN Community Domain AI Opportunity Engine Brief - 2026-09-13

## Product decision

The AI opportunity engine is a paid Community Domain command-centre capability. It is not a stream chat, not a member chat room, and not a replacement for WhatsApp conversation.

Working label: `Opportunity Engine`.

Placement: Community Domain Command Centre.

Owner value: keep a community, business, church, association, NGO, marketplace, or local institution ahead of changing needs by reading structured GSN activity plus cited external signals and turning them into opportunity, risk, and next-step intelligence.

## Core thesis

GSN should not sell this as `AI chat`. It should sell this as paid `community intelligence` and `opportunity foresight` attached to a Community Domain.

A community already produces signals:

- what members ask for in DemandBox
- what shops publish and fail to sell
- what support requests repeat
- what events, notices, and programmes recur
- what trust and completion evidence accumulates
- what people ignore, delay, or respond to quickly
- what goods/services appear informally before they become businesses

The Opportunity Engine turns those signals into structured intelligence.

## First paid module shape

The first screen should be a Community Domain command-centre lane with these tabs or filters:

- `Signals` - what changed inside this Community Domain recently
- `Political` - public-policy, governance, institutional, civic, or leadership implications
- `Economic` - income, demand, pricing, jobs, supply, affordability, spending, funding, and business opportunities
- `Social` - household needs, community behaviour, education, faith, age groups, migration, wellbeing, and informal support patterns
- `Technology` - tools, automation, digital access, AI, payment, phone, media, and operational improvements
- `Legal` - regulation, permissions, contracts, safeguarding, record duties, data, and compliance risks
- `Environmental` - weather, place, transport, housing condition, energy, waste, local environment, and resilience
- `Opportunities` - commercial or support opportunities GSN thinks are emerging
- `Risks` - what could go wrong if the domain ignores the signals
- `Next Moves` - suggested actions framed as options, not instructions

## Output card contract

Each AI output card should include:

- `Signal` - what GSN observed
- `Evidence` - which internal records or external sources support the observation
- `Interpretation` - what it may mean, using cautious language
- `Opportunity` - what could be built, sold, organised, tested, or funded
- `Risk` - what could go wrong or be misunderstood
- `Time horizon` - now, 90 days, 1 year, 5 years, or 10 years
- `Confidence` - low, medium, or high
- `Suggested next step` - one practical action the owner may choose
- `Human review` - owner/admin confirms before publishing or acting

## Data boundary

The engine must use structured GSN records first. External information must be cited and treated as supporting context, not secret knowledge. AI suggestions must not pretend to be legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, political instruction, guarantee, diagnosis, or proof of demand.

## Paid-product boundary

This should be a paid Community Domain intelligence capability because it can create recurring owner value:

- monthly opportunity brief
- weekly community signal scan
- donor/sponsor readiness brief
- shop/service opportunity report
- programme design suggestions
- early warning report
- market gap report
- community needs evidence pack

## Devil-truth boundary

This feature can become more valuable than ordinary shop listings if it reliably helps owners see opportunity before competitors. It can also become dangerous if it hallucinates, exposes private records, gives regulated advice, or turns weak activity signals into false certainty. The UI must present it as evidence-led suggestions requiring human judgment.

## 2. The 20-Minute Shape

```text
0-2 minutes: Respect, context, no-sales boundary
2-6 minutes: How ICA works today
6-10 minutes: One real ICA decision and recent example
10-13 minutes: Authority, privacy, refusal risk
13-18 minutes: Brief GSN explanation, four-story choice, and three-engine fit test
18-20 minutes: Feedback sheet, tester invitation, stop
```

Hard rule:

```text
At minute 13, move to GSN.
At minute 20, stop.
The final 5 minutes are product discovery, not a full feature presentation.
```

## 3. The 45-Second GSN Explanation

Use this only after ICA has answered Part A.

```text
GSN is community operating infrastructure, not another chat group.

It does not create trust for ICA, score people, replace executives, or replace
WhatsApp. It helps an association preserve selected evidence of membership,
contribution, service, support, commerce, and responsibility, with clear
confirmation, privacy, correction, and access rules.

For ICA, the question is whether GSN can help keep important notices, dues,
welfare support, member businesses, referrals, and opportunities from being
buried or forgotten.

The goal is to support human decisions and help ICA turn existing community
value into stronger support and opportunity.
```

---

## 4. The Three GSN Engines

Community Value Maintenance:

```text
Keeps ICA value from disappearing: official notices, meeting purpose/agenda,
member responses, summaries, decisions, attendance records, dues purpose,
support requests, contributions, businesses, skills, Demand Box needs, Spotlight,
and association memory.
```

Verification And Trust Transport:

```text
Lets confirmed evidence move carefully: membership, contribution, presence or
attendance evidence, service, trusted commerce, guarantor/reference support,
repayment history, Trust Passport, TrustSlip, and correction paths.
```

Opportunity Engine:

```text
Turns organised trust into practical opportunity: jobs, referrals, grants,
contracts, Demand Box requests, Spotlight exposure, business visibility, trusted
services, partnerships, and stronger community impact.
```

Ask first:

```text
Which pain is strongest for ICA today?
[ ] Administrative pain: meetings, notices, responses, decisions, records
[ ] Verification pain: membership, references, movement, trust evidence
[ ] Economic opportunity pain: demand, businesses, referrals, jobs, commerce
[ ] Cultural continuity/community memory pain: events, service, identity, handover, grant evidence
```

Then ask:

```text
Which one of these engines feels most useful for ICA first?
```

Do not read the full differentiation table during the 20-minute conversation.
Use the participant paper as the take-home proof that GSN is not merely being
compared with WhatsApp, but with the wider stack of chat, calendars, documents,
spreadsheets, forms, project tools, finance notes, CRMs, and manual memory.

## 5. What Makes It An Excellent Session

A strong 20-minute session produces:

- one painful ICA decision;
- the most recent example;
- where evidence currently lives;
- who should confirm the evidence;
- what must stay private;
- what would make ICA refuse;
- whether executives see day-to-day value;
- whether members see personal benefit;
- who is willing to join GSN as an early ICA tester;
- which current capability they want to test now and which roadmap capability
  they care about later.

A weak session produces:

- compliments;
- broad feature requests;
- no named decision;
- no privacy concern;
- no authority model;
- no one willing to test.

## 6. The Intelligent Signup Ask

Say this at the end:

```text
I am not asking ICA to adopt everything today. I am asking for honest early
testers. If you can see one useful ICA workflow, join the GSN app as a tester
and tell us what is useful, confusing, risky, or missing.
```

Track the answer:

```text
[ ] Executive/admin tester
[ ] Ordinary member tester
[ ] Business/opportunity tester
[ ] Welfare/support tester
[ ] Wants link but not now
[ ] Not interested yet
```

## 7. Tomorrow's Devil's Advocate

Do not hide from these truths:

```text
If they do not see economic or personal benefit, they will not keep using it.
If executives think it adds work, they will abandon it.
If members think it is politics, surveillance, or gossip, they will resist it.
If GSN cannot support one small ICA workflow clearly, the story is still too broad.
If you present silent Bluetooth attendance, provider messaging, or automatic
bank reconciliation as already finished, you will weaken trust. QR attendance
and explicit browser Bluetooth/proximity check-in are now built as Presence
Evidence, but they still do not prove contribution, location, or trust by
themselves.
```

Your strongest posture:

```text
GSN is not here to rescue ICA.
GSN is here to test which existing ICA pain is worth organising first: meetings
and administration, verification and member proof, economic opportunity, or
cultural continuity and community memory.
```

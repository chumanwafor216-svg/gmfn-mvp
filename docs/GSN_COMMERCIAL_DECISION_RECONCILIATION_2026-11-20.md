# GSN Commercial Decision Reconciliation

Date: 20 November 2026
Status: working management record; implementation and evidence boundaries remain explicit

## Purpose

This record prevents GSN from repeatedly reopening commercial choices that have already been made while also preventing recommendations and unproven market assumptions from being misrepresented as settled facts.

Four statuses are used:

- Settled working decision: use it for design, implementation planning and conditional modelling unless the founder deliberately changes it.
- Implementation pending: the product direction is decided, but code, operations, controls or migration are incomplete.
- Recommendation awaiting adoption: a reasoned proposal, not yet a founder-approved rule.
- Evidence question: answer through observed use, cost or payment behaviour rather than another abstract management debate.

## Settled Spotlight commercial contract

The working contract is:

> One GBP 0.50 Pipeline Credit purchases 30 scheduled Spotlight rotation opportunities for one eligible advert in its authorized community rotation on one campaign day: ten morning, ten afternoon and ten evening.

The service does not promise:

- a fixed number of different people;
- a minimum of three views;
- a click, enquiry, sale or completed transaction;
- endorsement, verification or trust;
- distribution to six communities merely because an audience example used six communities;
- a ten-second completed playback to every viewer.

The service does measure:

- scheduled opportunities promised and supplied;
- qualifying impressions;
- unique recognized reach;
- frequency;
- contact actions and later protected outcomes where lawfully linked;
- missed delivery, adjustments and remedies.

## Settled public-discovery direction

Publishing an active offer deliberately to Public Shop makes it eligible for relevant public search by default.

Owner controls remain essential:

- hide or pause discovery;
- withdraw unavailable offers;
- distinguish hidden-from-search from private or direct-link-only behavior;
- explain publication consequences before the write;
- preserve private contacts, private evidence, protected records and exact private location.

Existing Shop records require a migration and communication decision. The settled default for new deliberate public publication must not be silently imposed on ambiguous historical records.

## Canonical commercial rail

Ordinary merchant Spotlight should use Pipeline Credits as the customer-facing service unit:

1. reconcile payment;
2. award purchased credits once;
3. quote the package and check capacity;
4. create the entitlement;
5. schedule and record supplied opportunities;
6. link adjustments to the original purchase;
7. provide the delivery report.

Institutional invoices or sponsored programmes may fund the same entitlement system without forcing every payer through identical checkout. They must not create a second Spotlight ledger, wallet or delivery definition.

Live payment remains suspended until the authoritative rail, accounting, capacity, fulfillment, remedy and reporting controls are connected and verified.

## Forecast correction

The working tariff and package are sufficiently defined for a conditional five-year promotion scenario.

That scenario must expose:

- planned launch date;
- eligible active merchants;
- paying proportion;
- credits per paying merchant;
- repeat purchase and retention;
- available inventory;
- refunds and adjustments;
- direct delivery, support, moderation and acquisition cost;
- payment timing;
- tax and community-share assumptions.

A conservative operating/base case may still hold promotion revenue at zero until evidence gates pass. The conditional case and the conservative case should coexist rather than treating either as certain.

## Matters that remain genuinely open

- authoritative community distribution scope for one credit;
- multi-community pricing and entitlement behavior;
- service-day timezone and daylight-saving treatment;
- late-booking cutoff and partial-day products;
- cancellation, underdelivery, make-good and refund policy;
- overnight allocation;
- VAT presentation and future non-GBP tariffs;
- safe saleable capacity below theoretical maximum;
- treatment and communication for existing public Shop records;
- exact Community Domain package obligations and recurring price;
- observed willingness to pay, repeat purchase, support cost and contribution;
- actual institutional buyer, budget and contract;
- measured receipt and TrustSlip preparation costs;
- whether matching beats simpler search or human assistance;
- purpose-specific external reuse of TrustSlip.

## Matters that should not be reopened without a deliberate founder change

- GBP 0.50 working price per Pipeline Credit;
- 30 scheduled opportunities per credit;
- 10/10/10 daypart allocation;
- scheduled opportunity as the purchased unit;
- impressions and unique reach as measured outcomes;
- no five-person target or ceiling;
- five qualifying occasions as five impressions;
- no viewer-counter reset after additional purchases;
- ten seconds as the scheduling calculation baseline;
- Pipeline Credits as the standard ordinary-merchant direction;
- payment never buying trust, endorsement or a favorable conclusion.

## Truth boundary

These decisions define what GSN intends to sell and how to model it conditionally. They do not prove customer demand, contribution margin, scalable delivery, repeat purchase, institutional acceptance or a break-even date.

No product behavior, API, schema, payment state, production data or deployment is changed by this record.

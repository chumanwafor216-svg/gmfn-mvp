## 1. Purpose

This pack turns Chuma's LinkedIn profile, the GSN LinkedIn Page, Facebook Page,
and founder outreach into one clear invitation:

```text
GSN - Community value, carried forward.
Explore a community pilot.
```

The goal is not to look busy on social media. The goal is to get community
leaders, association executives, churches, charities, diaspora networks,
support organisations, and umbrella bodies to take one concrete next step:
submit a safe expression of interest or email GSN with enough information to
start a leadership discussion.

### 8.3 Reply When Someone Says "What Is GSN?"

```text
GSN is community trust infrastructure. It helps communities preserve useful evidence of membership, participation, support, contribution and opportunity so that community value does not disappear when people move, leaders change or records are scattered.

It does not replace community judgement. It helps organise evidence so leaders, members and receiving organisations can make better-informed decisions.

The current pilot route is here:
https://globalsupportnetwork.org/#pilot-interest
```

## 10. First-Response Triage After An Enquiry Arrives

Classify each enquiry within 24 hours.

Use one label:

```text
[ ] Strong pilot candidate - clear organisation, authority path and repeated workflow.
[ ] Warm discovery lead - real interest, but authority or workflow still unclear.
[ ] Needs clarification - missing membership, role, contact or problem detail.
[ ] Not suitable now - no governed community/workflow fit, or asks for risky data use.
[ ] Partner/sponsor route - not a pilot community, but may support, fund, refer or advise.
```

Reply with one next action:

- request missing details;
- offer a 20-minute discovery call;
- ask who has authority to approve a pilot;
- suggest starting with one branch/chapter;
- park politely if no fit exists.

## 12. Weekly Posting Rhythm

Do not post random repeated adverts. Use one clear theme per post.

Week 1:

- Post 1: main invitation.
- Post 2: why community value disappears when records are scattered.
- Post 3: examples of communities that can start with one branch/chapter.

Week 2:

- Post 1: what a controlled pilot looks like.
- Post 2: what GSN does not collect at first contact.
- Post 3: invitation reminder with the pilot-interest link.

Week 3:

- Post 1: founder reflection on why community evidence matters.
- Post 2: anonymised workflow example, if a real conversation has created one.
- Post 3: call for umbrella associations and support organisations.

## 13. What Not To Claim

Do not claim:

- that GSN has onboarded a large community unless there is written proof;
- that a pilot is approved because someone liked the idea;
- that GBP 25/month buys unlimited large-community rollout;
- that GSN replaces leadership judgement;
- that GSN verifies every claim automatically;
- that GSN is a bank, credit bureau, government verifier or grant provider;
- that private records should be uploaded before safeguards and scope are agreed;
- that Facebook likes, shares or profile views are pilot traction.

Count only stronger evidence:

- named community;
- named decision-maker;
- named administrator;
- painful repeated workflow;
- agreed demo or pilot discussion date;
- payment, sponsor or budget-holder route;
- written next action.

## 15. Devil's Advocate

The website route is now good enough to invite interest, but it is not enough to
prove demand. The proof will come from who responds, whether they have authority,
whether the workflow is painful, and whether they will consider payment,
sponsorship or written commitment after a useful pilot.

The next danger is vanity activity: posts, likes, views and polite comments. Use
social media only to create leadership conversations and decision-grade evidence.

## Purpose

This document replaces the old public habit of presenting GSN as a fixed-number capability list.

Use **GSN Core Capabilities** for public-facing app surfaces, executive summaries, first conversations, and ordinary client/member explanation. The larger `GSN in Real Life` bank remains the controlled setup source for deeper rollout, audience selection, upgrades, operator work and Strategic Application Lenses.

Devil truth: this list explains durable product value. It is not a promise that every capability is live for every client, and it does not expose every internal module GSN can use.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Recognised Community Identity**  
   GSN gives a real group a governed identity beyond WhatsApp names, paper lists and one person's phone.

2. **Governed Membership and Roles**  
   GSN separates leaders, admins, members, visitors and public readers so access follows responsibility.

3. **Invite and QR Entry**  
   GSN helps people enter the correct community, document, shop or setup flow without typing links manually.

4. **Official Announcements and Response Evidence**  
   GSN keeps official notices, acknowledgement, availability and follow-up evidence from being buried in chat noise.

5. **Meetings, Attendance and Decision Memory**  
   GSN connects meetings, attendance, decisions, files, action owners and handover memory into one readable trail.

6. **TrustPassport and TrustSlip Evidence**  
   GSN lets members carry bounded identity and trust evidence without pretending trust is automatic proof.

7. **Community Marketplace and Public Shop**  
   GSN gives shops, services, products, media, Spotlight, contacts and verification entry points one organised public home.

8. **DemandBox and Ask Community**  
   GSN routes needs, offers, requests and local demand into a structured lane while keeping official bulletin space protected.

9. **Support, Welfare and Contribution Records**  
   GSN makes welfare, support, contribution, repayment and responsibility records easier to review without turning GSN into a bank.

10. **Opportunity and Activity Analytics**  
    GSN helps leaders read attention, demand, participation, Spotlight movement, shop activity and opportunity signals without mistaking signals for proof.

11. **Documents, Downloads and Bridges**  
    GSN packages setup papers, QR download sheets, public links, private files and approved bridges so people receive the right material.

12. **Continuity, Handover and Institutional Memory**  
    GSN keeps community identity, roles, documents, decisions, evidence, setup history and handover usable after leaders or devices change.

## Audience Use

Use the full core set for GSN positioning and app surfaces. For client distribution, choose only the few that fit the organisation:

- Churches and faith groups: identity, membership, announcements, meetings, welfare, documents and handover.
- Schools and youth groups: identity, roles, QR entry, announcements, attendance, documents and handover.
- NGOs and charities: identity, roles, announcements, project memory, support records, documents and analytics.
- Cooperatives, markets and business networks: identity, roles, marketplace/shop, DemandBox, support records, analytics and continuity.
- Families, diaspora groups and associations: identity, roles, QR entry, trust evidence, support records, documents and handover.

## Controlled Full Bank

The expanded `GSN in Real Life` bank preserves the 44 original modules and adds Strategic Application Lenses, but it should not be the default public front.

Use the full bank only when:
- the setup team is designing a rollout
- a client leader needs the deeper map
- an audience-specific copy must be prepared
- an upgrade changes the setup pack
- due diligence requires a fuller explanation

Do not send the full bank to ordinary members when a smaller audience copy is enough.
